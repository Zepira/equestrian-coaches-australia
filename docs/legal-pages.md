## 2. Privacy policy

**title:** Privacy policy
**updated:** 12 October 2026
**intro:** What we collect, why, where it's kept, who else handles it, and what you can ask us to do with it. We follow the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988, whether or not the law requires a business our size to. We don't sell or rent anyone's details, and we don't show anyone ads based on who they are.

### Who this covers

Equine Professionals Australia (Alana La Bouchardiere and Kim Thompson in partnership, ABN 74 931 177 211). Privacy questions and requests go to {contact_email}.

### If you're looking for help

To search, you don't need an account and we don't ask who you are. If you press "Use my location", your browser asks your permission, we turn the coordinates into the nearest suburb to run the search, and we don't keep the coordinates.

If you make an account we keep your name, email address, the professionals you save, and any alerts you set up: the suburb or postcode, how far to look, and what you follow.

When you send an enquiry, your name, how to contact you (email, and phone if you give it), your suburb and your message go to that professional, and we keep a copy so you can both see where it's up to. A few weeks later we may email you once to ask whether you booked and whether you'd like to leave a review.

If you leave a review, we publish your first name, the first letter of your surname, your rating, your words and the date. We keep your email address and a scrambled code for your device so one person can't review the same professional twice; neither is ever shown.

If you enter a competition, we keep your name, email address, state, your answer and any photo you send, for the length of the competition and 12 months after, so we can run it and show we ran it fairly. A winner's first name, surname initial and state are published.

If you download a guide, join the waitlist or sign up to alerts, we keep your email address and a record of what you agreed to.

### If you list with us

We keep what you put on your profile: your name, business name, where you're based and how far you travel, what you do, qualifications, registration numbers and insurance details you choose to add, photos, video, testimonials and events. Your email address and phone number are only shown to visitors if you switch them on, and even then behind a click, not printed on the page.

We also keep your account email, your plan and its payment history, the enquiries sent to you, the reviews about you and your replies, referral codes you've shared, and which pages of the site your profile appeared on. Stripe handles your card and your billing details; we see what was charged, never the card number.

### What we count, and why

We count how often each profile appears in search, is opened, has a number revealed and gets an enquiry, so professionals can see their numbers and so we can tell them honestly whether the site is working. To count each visitor once a day we make a scrambled code from the visitor's connection details and a daily key; we don't store the connection details, and the code can't be reversed. We don't count a professional looking at their own profile, or us.

We record what people search for and how many results they got, without who searched, so we can see what the site is missing.

We use Google Search Console to see which searches bring people to the site. That's about pages, not people, and Google doesn't give us anyone's identity.

### Cookies and your browser

The site uses three cookies and nothing else. One keeps you signed in. Two remember which link brought you here (a poster, a partner, a post) for 90 days, so we can tell which ways of reaching people work. Your browser also keeps a few preferences of its own, like whether you hid the map. We don't use advertising cookies, analytics trackers, tracking pixels or any other company's trackers, and we don't need a cookie banner because nothing here tracks you. The full list is at /cookies.

### Emails and texts

We only send marketing, including alerts, round-ups, invitations and news, to people who agreed to it, and we keep a record of each agreement: what you agreed to, the exact words you saw, where and when. Every marketing email says who sent it and has a link to change what you get or stop everything, which works for at least 30 days and takes effect within five business days (usually straight away). Emails about your own account, like an enquiry sent to you, a review about you, a receipt or a password reset, keep coming while the account is open. If an email to you bounces or is marked as spam, we stop emailing that address.

If we offer text message alerts, they'll be opt-in, from a sender name registered with the ACMA, and you'll be able to stop them by replying STOP.

### Sponsors and referrals

Some pages and emails carry a slot labelled "Sponsored". Every visitor sees the same slot; nothing about you is used to choose it, and sponsors never see who you are. If you use a referral link, we record that the person who signed up came from it so the referrer gets their reward, and nothing else about either of you is shared.

### Decisions made automatically

Some things the site decides on its own. Search results are ordered by distance and by how well someone matches what you searched for; how that works is on our "How the list is ordered" page. A professional's profile is hidden automatically when their payment fails and comes back when it's paid. A review is held for a person to read if it looks like a duplicate or came from the same device as another review of the same professional. A person looks at every new profile before it goes live, and a person makes every decision to remove a profile or a review. No decision about you is made from your personal details beyond where you are and what you searched for.

### Who helps us run the site, and where your details go

The details above are stored with Supabase, whose servers for us are in Sydney, Australia. The site is hosted by Vercel, which runs it from servers in the United States and at network points around the world. Payments go through Stripe, in the United States. Email is sent through Resend, from servers in Japan. Maps come from OpenFreeMap, which sees the map area you look at but not who you are. Search data comes from Google Search Console.

These companies handle your details only to provide their service to us, under contracts that stop them using it for anything else. Some of them hold it outside Australia, which means it's covered by the law of the country it's in. We take reasonable steps to make sure each of them protects it the way the Australian Privacy Principles require. Beyond those companies, we only disclose your details to a professional you contact, to a rider you reply to, or where the law requires it.

### How we protect it

Everything travels over HTTPS. The database is only reachable by our own server code, with a separate key for each kind of access, and each person's data is locked to their own account by rules in the database itself. Card numbers never touch our systems. Both of us use two-factor sign-in on every service that holds your details. If we ever have a breach that's likely to cause you serious harm, we'll tell you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner as soon as we can.

### How long we keep it

For as long as your account is open. When an account is closed, its profile, alerts, saved professionals and enquiries go with it within 30 days. An enquiry's name, contact details and message are deleted {enquiry_months} months after it was sent, whether or not the accounts are still open; the professional's count of enquiries stays. Reviews stay published after the reviewer's account closes, because they're about the professional, unless you ask us to remove yours. We keep billing records for five years, as tax law requires, and consent records for as long as we might need to show what you agreed to. Backups clear within 30 days.

### Seeing, changing or deleting your details

You can change most of your details yourself from your account or dashboard. To see everything we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted, email {contact_email}. We'll confirm who you are, then reply within 30 days. We'll delete everything we can; a few things have to stay (billing records, and anything we need for a dispute that's already started), and we'll tell you what and why.

### Children

The site is for adults. We don't knowingly collect details from anyone under 18, and a parent who enquires about lessons for a child gives us their own details, not the child's. If you think a child has given us their details, email {contact_email} and we'll delete them.

### Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your details, email {contact_email} and we'll reply within 14 days and try to fix it. If we can't, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au or 1300 363 992.

### Changes to this policy

When we change it, the new version goes here with its date. If the change matters, we'll email account holders.

## 3. Cookies

New block `legal.cookies`, page at /cookies. Australia has no cookie-banner law; what the Privacy Act asks is that people can find out what the site stores on their device, and that's this page.

**title:** Cookies and what the site stores on your device
**updated:** 12 October 2026
**intro:** The whole list. There's no banner because there's nothing to agree to: the site doesn't track you, and none of these go to anyone but us.

### Cookies we set

Signed in: a cookie from our sign-in service (named `sb-...`) that keeps you logged in. Set when you log in, gone when you log out or after a week of not visiting. Without it you can't stay signed in.

Where you came from: `epa_ft` and `epa_lt`. If you arrive through one of our links, from a poster's QR code, a partner's page or a post, they remember which one for 90 days: the first one ever and the latest one. They hold the link's name, not anything about you. We use them to see which ways of reaching people work. If you then make an account or sign up to alerts, we copy that link name onto your record.

Test site only: a password cookie on our test address, which the public site never sets.

### What your browser keeps for us

A few preferences that stay on your device and never leave it: whether you hid the map, the last suburb you searched, which tab you had open in your dashboard. Clear your browser's site data to remove them.

### What we don't use

No analytics service. No advertising cookies. No tracking pixels in the site or in our emails. No social media buttons that report back. No other company's cookies at all. If that changes, this page changes first.

### Turning cookies off

You can block cookies in your browser. The site still works for searching; you won't be able to stay signed in.

## 4. Cancelling and refunds

New block `legal.refunds`, page at /refunds. **title:** Cancelling, changing and refunds
**updated:** 12 October 2026
**intro:** For professionals with a paid listing. Riders and horse owners never pay us anything.

### How billing works

Your plan is charged in advance, monthly or yearly, to the card you gave Stripe, and renews automatically until you cancel. The price you see on the pricing page is the whole price; there's no GST while we're not registered, and no setup or cancellation fee. You get a receipt from Stripe for every charge.

### Cancelling

Dashboard, Billing, Cancel. Two clicks, no email to us, no phone call. Cancelling stops the next renewal. Your profile stays live until the end of the period you've already paid for, then comes down. You can undo a cancellation any time before that date.

### Changing plan

Up: the change happens now, you pay the difference for the rest of the period, and the new features switch on straight away. Down: the change happens at the end of the current period, you keep what you paid for until then, and nothing is deleted. Extra photos, your second location or past events go dormant and come back if you upgrade again.

Going up for one month and back down the next is fine. That's what the Clinic plan is for.

### Pausing

You can pause your listing for up to {pause_max_months} months from the dashboard. Nothing is charged while it's paused and your profile is hidden; your numbers, reviews and photos wait for you.

### Refunds

We don't refund part of a month or year you've already started, because your profile has been live for it. We do refund in full, to the card you paid with, within ten business days, if:

- we charged you by mistake, or charged the wrong amount;
- we remove something from your plan mid-period and you cancel because of it (the unused part);
- we end your listing for a reason that isn't a breach of the terms (the unused part);
- the site was down for more than seven days in a row during a period you paid for (that period);
- the law says we have to.

To ask, email {contact_email} with the receipt. If you dispute a charge with your bank instead, tell us too, so we can sort it out faster.

### Founding members

You pay nothing until {free_months} months after the site opens to riders. We email you the date when it's set, then 30, 14 and 3 days before the first charge. Cancel any time before it and you're never charged. After it, the rules above apply, at {founding_price} a month for as long as you stay.

### If a payment fails

Stripe tries again over the next few days and emails you. If it still fails, your profile is hidden until you update the card from your dashboard, then it comes straight back. Nothing is deleted.

## 5. Review policy additions

Two sections to add to the existing `review_policy` block. The existing sections stay as they are.

### If a review is about you and you think it's defamatory or false

Press "Report" on the review, or email {contact_email} with the link to the review and what you say is wrong in it. Tell us which parts are untrue and why. We'll acknowledge it within two business days and give you a reasonable response within seven days of receiving enough detail to act on: we'll take it down, hold it while we ask the reviewer to confirm what they wrote, or tell you why it stays and that you can reply publicly under it. We don't take down a review just because it's negative, and we don't edit anyone's words. Reviewers are told when a review they wrote is reported, and we keep a record of every report and what we did.

### Incentives, relationships and disclosure

Nobody is paid or rewarded for a review, by us or by the professional, and a professional who offers anything for a review loses their listing. A reviewer's relationship to a professional (family, employee, business partner, competitor) has to be declared, and a review with an undeclared one comes down. Paying us for a plan changes nothing about reviews: not which show, not their order, not the average. Every rating shows how many reviews it's made from.
