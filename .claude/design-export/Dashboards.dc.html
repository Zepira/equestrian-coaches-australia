Turn 1Coach dashboard & rider account — Golden Hour direction

Logged-in pages, so the drama drops and the numbers lead. The coach dashboard puts the monthly figures from the For Coaches promise front and centre — impressions, views, reveals, enquiries — with an enquiry inbox, a taking-students switch, profile completeness and the founding-offer billing state. Tabs work in both frames. The rider account is deliberately small: favourites, clinic alerts, and the enquiries they've sent.
