1Homepage — three visual treatments · same palette, new type, new motion
What I kept, what I changed

Kept: the Paddock Edit palette (cream, hunter green, terracotta, sand tint), the arch as a photo signature, the two-audience homepage, the mock coach roster and the Unsplash discipline set already in the codebase.

Changed: Fraunces → Instrument Serif (sharper, more fashion, italic used as the emphasis voice); Work Sans → Hanken Grotesk. Copy rewritten warm and plain. Hero built for phones first: headline draws in word by word, the photo drifts slower than the page, the search reacts as you type — try typing a town (Bendigo, Toowoomba, Orange…) in any frame.

Frames scroll like a real phone/laptop so you can feel the scroll reveals and parallax. Toggle Full page in Tweaks to unroll them. Also: search results · coach profile · current site for reference.

The three directions

1a Golden Hour — cinematic. Full-bleed photo, deep green scrim, glass search card that rises from the bottom edge on phones. Closest to today, dialled up.

1b The Journal — light editorial. Cream hero, the search is a sentence you finish ("I ride ___ near ___"), tall arched portrait, discipline ticker.

1c Night Arena — search-first, all-green. The headline cycles through disciplines, the location field is the hero, photography arrives as a film strip below. Boldest of the three.
