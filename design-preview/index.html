What happens next

Pick a direction, not a page.

Choose one direction and, within it, one palette. You can also tell us to take the type from one and the device from another — that is a normal outcome at this stage.

Once a direction is chosen we build the full homepage in it, then the coach profile, search results and the coach sign-up flow. The photography brief is written from the direction you pick, so real shoots can start in parallel.

Feedback deadline: Friday 4 September 2026. Comments in one reply are easier to act on than several — a short note per direction is plenty.

Notes on the content
Every discipline is searchable from launch. Liberty, bridleless and working equitation lead the front page because they are where the directory is strongest and where riders have the hardest time finding a coach.
Coach names, towns, distances and one-line bios are written as sample content so the pages read like the real thing. Real listings are written and maintained by the coaches themselves.
Photography is stock stand-in only. The shot list for the direction you choose is written from its studio notes, so a real shoot can start in parallel.
Pricing is as briefed: $9.99 a month for a full profile, $14.95 with clinics and events.
Hero headline, subhead and both button labels are the draft copy from the brief and are still open to change.
