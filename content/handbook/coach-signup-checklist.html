Handbook Equine Professionals Australia

Before coaches sign up

What has to happen before the first real coach can join, in the order to do it, what can start this week, and what can safely wait until riders arrive.

Launch checklist, revision 1 27 September 2026 Tracked on Trello: "Before coaches sign up" Decisions for Kim, section 04
01

The short version

The build is further along than Trello said, but four things stop a real coach signing up today. The public site can only show the coming soon page. There is no Stripe account. The sign-up email breaks when it's opened in a different browser from the one the coach signed up in. And the terms still have blanks in them.

Everything under "Must" below comes to roughly two weekends of build work, plus a Stripe application, a bank account and a solicitor booking. Everything in section 09 can wait for riders: most of it is already built and simply switched off.

Every item here also has a card in the Trello list "Before coaches sign up", in the same order. Tick things off there; this document is the reasoning.

Found in the Trello sweep

The For Coaches page sells five features that aren't built yet: SMS enquiry alerts, Google search impressions and queries, the testimonial request link and reminders, and pinning a testimonial (all on Spotlight), and up to three locations (on Clinic). Charging for a feature that doesn't exist is a consumer-law problem. Take those lines off the page before anyone pays and add each one back when it ships.

03

The switch the site doesn't have yet

Right now "launched" is one switch, SITE_LAUNCHED. Off, the public domain shows only the coming soon page, so a coach can only sign up on the password-protected test site. On, the whole site opens to riders and Google at once. A coaches-first launch needs a setting in between.

It matters for Stripe too. Stripe won't activate an account whose website is password protected or under construction. It needs to see, publicly, what we sell, the prices, a way to contact us, and the refund and cancellation policy. The coming soon page on its own fails that check.

Recommended build, about half a day: a third stage, for example SITE_STAGE=coaches, that:

  • opens the coach paths on the public domain: /for-coaches, /for-professionals, /join/*, /signup, /login, /auth/*, /forgot-password, /reset-password, /onboarding, /dashboard/*, /terms, /privacy, /about and the Stripe webhook
  • lets a coach's published profile be seen and shared at /profile/[slug], kept noindex until riders open
  • keeps search, area pages, events and rider accounts behind the coming soon page
  • allows a live Stripe key in this stage (the guard in src/lib/stripe.ts refuses one unless the whole site is launched)
  • adds a "Coach or horse professional? Join now" link to the coming soon page

One more guard while you're in there. With no Stripe key set, billing runs in mock mode and hands out a real active plan for free. That's right on the test site and dangerous on the public one, so make mock mode refuse to run on the public host.

05

Must do before the first coach

In order. The business items gate Stripe, and Stripe's website review needs the coaches stage to be live.

1

The business (both of you)

  • Sign the partnership agreement. Until it's signed, the Partnership Act's default split applies, not the one you agreed. Sign before any money moves.
  • Open the business bank account in the partnership's name. Stripe pays out to it and won't activate without it.
  • Move Vercel to Pro ($20 a month) before the first payment. Hobby is licensed for non-commercial use only, and taking coach payments is plainly commercial. The call was "at launch"; coaches paying is launch.
  • Check the Supabase plan. Real coaches and payment records need backups you can restore. Pro ($25 a month) has daily backups; move to it if we're on Free.
  • Public liability quotes, then decide. As a partnership there's no company between a claim and our personal assets. The Trello card said revisit "on launch day", and this is that day.
2

Stripe (Alana)

  • Apply as the partnership under ABN 74 931 177 211, not as a sole trader, or the income lands as Alana's. Add Kim as a team member. Set a statement descriptor a coach will recognise on a bank statement, such as EQUINE PROS, which heads off "what's this charge" disputes.
  • Products and prices in test mode first: three products with a monthly and a yearly price each, plus the $9.99 founding price. Enter each pair in Admin, Plans and prices, which checks them against Stripe on save.
  • The webhook at /api/webhooks/stripe, sending five events: checkout.session.completed, customer.subscription.created, customer.subscription.updated, customer.subscription.deleted and invoice.paid. Its signing secret goes in STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET.
  • The Customer Portal (a dashboard setting): card updates and invoices on; switching allowed between the three plans only, never to the founding price; upgrades immediate and prorated, downgrades at the end of the period.
  • Smart Retries, failed-payment emails, card-expiry emails and receipts switched on. All dashboard settings.
  • The 100%-off-once coupon for referrals, with its ID in Admin, Codes and referrals.
  • No GST anywhere. We're not registered (turnover is under $75,000), so nothing should read like a tax invoice. Leave Stripe Tax off and ask John Saxon to check the invoice wording.
  • Then repeat it all in live mode. Live products have different IDs, and test and live share one database, so swap the Admin price pairs to the live IDs in the same sitting as the keys.
  • Fix the net-price figure in Admin. It uses 1.7% plus 30c but leaves out Stripe Billing's 0.7% of subscription volume, so $9.99 nets about $9.45, not $9.52.
3

Build blockers (Alana)

  • The coaches stage and the mock-payments guard, from section 03.
  • The email confirmation fix, the first item in section 06. Without it a large share of coaches will fail to confirm.
  • Supabase Auth email through Resend. Supabase's built-in mailer only delivers to our own team's addresses, two an hour. If custom SMTP isn't set in the Supabase dashboard, no coach gets a confirmation email. Set the production Site URL and redirect URLs at the same time.
  • Wipe and rebuild the database before the first real coach, not on rider launch day as docs/launch.md has it. Export the waitlist CSV first. After this the wipe script must never run again, so make it refuse once a real provider exists.
  • Video upload will fail. It allows 40 MB through a server action, but Next.js caps server-action bodies at 1 MB by default and Vercel caps request bodies at 4.5 MB. Founding coaches get Spotlight, which includes video, so they will try it. Upload straight to Supabase Storage with a signed upload URL. Photos are fine once resized, except an iPhone HEIC file the browser can't read, which falls back to the full-size original and hits the same cap.
  • Friendly error pages. There's a 404 page but no error.tsx or global-error.tsx, so a crash shows Next's "Application error" screen.
  • Error alerts. Nothing tells us when a coach hits an error; we'd hear from Kim a week later. Sentry's free tier or a Vercel log alert is enough.
4

Legal and content

  • Take the five unbuilt features off the For Coaches page (section 01).
  • Fill the blanks in the terms: the partnership name and ABN, the governing law (Victoria, as in the agreement) and the liability wording.
  • Add a refund line to the terms. They cover cancelling but never say "no refunds for part periods", and Stripe requires a stated refund policy.
  • The solicitor on the terms and privacy policy. The draft banner says "our solicitor is checking it before the site opens", which stops being true the day coaches pay. Book them now; if the review lands after opening, reword the banner.
  • Admin, Settings: the ABN, review_alert_emails (both of us, or new profiles sit unseen in review), show_sample_listings off, and legal_approved once the solicitor signs off.
  • A phone number on every coach page. Stripe requires a public contact method, and a coach who gets stuck will ring rather than email. Kim's mobile, or a number we both answer, on For Coaches, the sign-up page and onboarding.

The test plan

Two halves: break it on purpose with Stripe's test tools, then watch real people use it without helping. The real Stripe paths (saving a card, the founding free period, launch-day subscriptions) have only ever run in mock mode, so the first half isn't optional.

  • The same with the 0341 card: the charge fails, the plan goes past due, the profile hides, and it comes back when the card is updated.

  • Cancel, undo the cancel, pause, resume, switch to yearly.

  • The same email twice; a wrong password; forgot password; a confirmation link opened on another device; a link more than a day old.
  • Leave onboarding halfway and come back the next day on another device.
  • A photo straight from an iPhone camera, a HEIC file on a Windows PC, a 20 MB photo, a video.
  • Extend scripts/launch/host-gate.mjs for the coaches stage: coach pages open on the public domain, rider pages still show coming soon, profiles carry noindex.

Three to five real people, watched

  • Pick people who aren't comfortable online and haven't seen the site: two coaches Kim trusts and one other. Not Kim; she knows it too well.
  • Send each the exact message Kim will send, on their own phone. Watch in person or on a video call with screen sharing.
  • Don't help. Write down every pause, wrong tap and question. Aim for under 15 minutes from the message to a submitted profile.
  • Fix the three worst moments, then run it once more with someone new.

Testers use the test site and the 4242 card, so nobody pays anything.

08

First month, while coaches arrive

Not blockers, but each gets harder or dearer the longer it waits.

  • Decide the database region before the wipe. Supabase is in Tokyo. The pre-opening wipe is the one free moment to rebuild in Sydney: faster pages for Australians, and simpler overseas-storage wording in the privacy policy. Once real coaches exist it's a migration.
  • Verify Search Console the day coach pages go public. Google only reports from the day it knows us, and "you appeared in Google 89 times" is the strongest number in the month-eight email.
  • Email the professionals on the waitlist that joining is open. Riders on the list agreed to one email at launch, so save theirs for when riders open. Check the waitlist's consent wording covers this first.
  • Decide which scheduled emails run in the coach phase. CRON_EMAILS_ENABLED is all or nothing. On, the monthly numbers email would tell every coach they had zero riders, which reads as failure. Keep that email off until riders open and send a short hand-written update instead. The "finish your profile" nudges are worth having now, so either make the switch per job or nudge by hand.
  • Review every profile within a day. Onboarding promises "usually within a day or two", and review alerts only arrive if review_alert_emails is set.
  • On Vercel Pro, run the sequences and campaigns jobs hourly. They're daily now because Hobby allows nothing faster.
  • Every account in the business's name, both of us as admins, one shared password manager: Stripe, Vercel, Supabase, Resend, GitHub, the registrar.
  • The DMARC record and the Resend bounce webhook, if they weren't done when Resend was set up.
  • The IP Australia trade mark search on "Equine Professionals Australia".
09

Can wait until riders

Most of this is built and switched off. Leave it off; none of it touches a coach signing up.

  • Setting launch_date (which starts every founding coach's free period), SITE_LAUNCHED=true, submitting the sitemap and emailing the rider waitlist: the launch-day list in docs/launch.md.
  • The rider monthly round-up, saved-search alerts, rider welcome emails and campaigns.
  • The weekly Search Console import and SEO digest. (Verifying the domain is in section 08.)
  • Written intros on the top 20 area pages; the twenty-query rank basket.
  • Featured slots (they need eight coaches in an area) and benchmarks (five with a month of numbers).
  • Competitions, sponsors, awards, guides and Riders' Choice.
  • The monthly numbers email to coaches, once there are riders to count.
  • Google and Facebook ads, the show stall and the QR cards. Agree the UTM scheme before anything is printed.
  • Pitch copy for each horse care profession, written after the first ten conversations.
  • Australia's subscription-trap rules, which start on 1 July 2027 and cover small-business subscriptions. The site already has one-click cancelling and renewal reminders; have the solicitor check the sign-up wording against them next year.
